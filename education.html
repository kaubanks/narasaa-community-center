Education that belongs to the community.

From the youngest learners to adult students, Narasaa strengthens pathways to confidence, literacy, and opportunity in Kimokouwa.

Sponsor a learner Volunteer as a teacher

Introduction

Learning is the foundation of lasting change

When children can start school ready, when teachers are supported, and when adults can keep learning, whole families gain ground.

Narasaa’s education work is practical and relational. We create welcoming learning spaces, support primary students, and open adult education for women and men who want new skills and stronger voices in community life.

Challenges

Barriers families still face

  • Distance, costs, and limited early learning options delay school readiness.
  • Primary students need tutoring, materials, and consistent encouragement.
  • Adults—especially women—often missed formal schooling opportunities.
  • Teachers need training, resources, and community partnership.

Our Approach

Community-rooted learning

We work with local teachers and leaders to design programs that fit Kimokouwa’s culture and daily rhythms.

  • Early childhood foundation
  • Primary student support
  • Adult literacy & skills
  • Teacher partnership
  • Family engagement

Programs

Learning at every stage

Baby Class

A gentle introduction to learning through play, language, routine, and care—helping the youngest children feel safe and curious.

Middle Class

Foundational reading, counting, confidence, and social skills that prepare children for primary school success.

Supporting Primary School Students

Targeted academic support, encouragement, and resources so students can stay engaged and progress with dignity.

Men’s Education

Practical learning for adult men—literacy, life skills, and community leadership that strengthens households and village decisions.

Women’s Education

When women learn, households rise

Women’s education at Narasaa builds literacy, confidence, practical skills, and leadership. It is one of the strongest investments a community can make.

Participants gain tools for daily life, income pathways, and a stronger voice in family and community decisions—while remaining rooted in Maasai identity and mutual support.

Support women’s learning

Teachers

The heart of every classroom

Narasaa honors teachers as professionals and partners. We support their work through collaboration, encouragement, and resources that make learning possible day after day.

Visitors and volunteers who teach do so under local guidance—respecting culture, language, and community priorities.

Success Stories

Progress you can feel

School readiness

Children who begin with baby and middle class arrive at primary school more confident and prepared to learn.

Adult confidence

Women and men report stronger literacy, communication, and participation in community life.

Teacher partnership

Local educators help shape curriculum rhythm and keep programs culturally grounded.

Future Goals

Where education is going

  • More learning materials and classroom support
  • Expanded adult education opportunities
  • Stronger teacher development partnerships
  • Scholarship-style support for primary students
