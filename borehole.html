Cleaner water. Stronger days.

Narasaa is advancing a borehole project so Kimokouwa families can spend less time searching for water—and more time on health, learning, and livelihood.

Support the water project Ask about impact

The Challenge

Current water realities in Kimokouwa

When water is far, uncertain, or unsafe, every part of life becomes harder—especially for women, children, and households caring for livestock.

Families may walk long distances, wait at limited sources, or rely on water that increases health risks. Scarcity also intensifies pressure during dry seasons, affecting school attendance, household work, and animal welfare.

Why Clean Water Matters

One project, many returns

H

Health benefits

Safer water reduces illness and the heavy costs of preventable disease on families.

E

Children & education

When fetching water takes less time and energy, children can focus on school and rest.

W

Women & families

Women often carry the water burden. Closer access restores time for work, learning, and leadership.

L

Livestock

Reliable water supports animals that remain central to livelihood and Maasai life.

C

Community development

Water security strengthens every other program—from education to enterprise.

F

Future plans

The borehole is a foundation for healthier growth across Kimokouwa for years to come.

Future Plans

Transparent progress, community stewardship

Narasaa approaches the borehole project with careful planning, local leadership, and donor transparency. Progress depends on partnership—financial support, technical collaboration, and community ownership.

  • Community consultation and site planning
  • Technical assessment and implementation phases
  • Local stewardship for long-term care
  • Updates shared with supporters
