Our story is a community story.

Narasaa Community Center grows from relationships in Kimokouwa—guided by local wisdom, shared responsibility, and hope that lasts.

Our Story

From village need to shared vision

Narasaa began with a simple conviction: lasting change must be led with the community, not for it.

In Kimokouwa Village, Longido District, families face real barriers—distance to learning opportunities, water insecurity, limited adult education, and pressure on cultural traditions in a changing world.

Narasaa Community Center brings people together around practical solutions: early childhood and school support, women’s and men’s learning, water security planning, cultural preservation, and tourism that treats visitors as guests and neighbors—not customers only.

Our Partnership with Simba Adventures

Safari with a Purpose

Experience Tanzania while helping communities thrive.

Narasaa Community Center partners with Simba Adventures, a Tanzania safari company, to connect travel with community support.

When you book a safari through Narasaa, a portion of the proceeds from your trip supports our community initiatives, including education, clean water, and other community development projects.

Your journey becomes part of something lasting: a memorable safari that also helps support the communities we serve.

Mission

What we do every day

To improve lives in Kimokouwa through education, clean water, women’s empowerment, community development, cultural preservation, and responsible tourism—always in partnership with local leadership.

Vision

Where we are headed

A thriving Kimokouwa where every child can learn, every family can access safer water, women lead with confidence, and Maasai culture is celebrated with pride by residents and respectful visitors alike.
