FAQ

Common questions

Who should I contact?

Contact Renée Danielle for donations, volunteering, education, and water projects. Contact Lebahati Keiya for safari planning, cultural tours, and tourism support.

How quickly will someone reply?

We aim to reply within a few business days. WhatsApp to Lebahati is usually fastest for travel questions.

Can I donate from outside Tanzania?

Yes. Use Renée’s GoFundMe link, or email narasaacommunitycenter@gmail.com for other options.

How do I book a cultural tour or safari?

Message Lebahati on WhatsApp or email, or use the form with Cultural Tour Booking / Safari Inquiry selected.
